### Danish Open Access Week 2026 - Oct 19-23

<img src="/OAW2026/images/oaw2026.jpg" alt="Open Access banner" style="height: 100%; width:100%;"/>

#### [Go directly to the programme](#programme-of-the-danish-open-access-week-2026) - [Practical information](#practical-information)

##### For the fifth year in a row, the Danish Open Access Network is very proud to invite everyone to the Danish Open Access Week 2026. Below you can read more about the [programme](#programme-of-the-danish-open-access-week-2026) and sign up for the individual webinars.

<p>The Danish Open Access Week 2026 will include a series of different webinars on Open Access. The programme has been designed to appeal to as broad an audience as possible and covers various aspects of Open Access.</p>
<p>The webinars are open to anyone with an interest in Open Access.
</p>

#### Practical information
<p>The webinars will be held on weekdays during week 43 (October 19-23 2026). You can read more about each webinar by clicking on the titles in the programme below. The registration form can be found under the description of each webinar. All the webinars are free of charge and anyone can register. All webinars are held in English.</p><p>The webinars will not be recorded. If the presenters give their consent, the slides will be shared after the webinars have taken place.</p>
