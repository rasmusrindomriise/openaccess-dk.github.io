### Danish Open Access Week 2026 - Oct 19-23

<img src="/OAW2026/images/oaw2026.jpg" alt="Open Access banner" style="height: 100%; width:100%;"/>

#### [Go directly to the programme](#programme-of-the-danish-open-access-week-2025) - [Practical information](#practical-information)

##### For the fifth year in a row, the Danish Open Access Network is very proud to invite everyone to the Danish Open Access Week 2026. Below you can read more about the [programme](#programme-of-the-danish-open-access-week-2025) and sign up for the individual webinars.

<p>The Danish Open Access Week 2026 will include a series of different webinars on Open Access. There will be presentations that explain the basics of Open Access and presentations that will look at the more complicated Open Access aspects.</p>
<p>The webinars are open to anyone with an interest in Open Access. Below you can read the week’s programme and register for each webinar.</p>

#### Practical information
<p>The webinars will be held on weekdays during week 43 (October 19-23 2026). For details about the individual webinars, see below. All the webinars are free of charge and anyone can sign up. All webinars are held in English.</p><p>For the sake of the climate and administration, we have decided not to record the webinars, but the slides will be made available on the site after the webinars have taken place.</p>
